# 📒 Engineering Logbook

The **handwritten logbook is the primary record.** This folder holds the digital version of each entry, plus scans of key handwritten pages in [`../media/logbook-scans/`](../media/).

## How entries are made (fast workflow)
1. During the day, jot rough notes in the paper logbook: what you tried, readings, what broke, what worked.
2. At the end of the session, send the rough notes (text or voice-to-text) for formatting.
3. Save the formatted entry here as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`.
4. Photograph the matching handwritten page into `media/logbook-scans/`.

## Rules that make a logbook credible
- **Date every entry.** Never go back and edit an old entry. Add a correction note in a new entry instead.
- **Record numbers, not adjectives:** "heading drifted 7° in 60 s", not "drift was bad".
- **Record failures.** They're the most useful part.
- **Label every claim:** as-built, calculated, estimated, or planned.
